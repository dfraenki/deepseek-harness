# DebateHarness Brand Asset Usage Guidelines

English

To maintain brand consistency for DebateHarness, please follow these specifications:

- Use "DebateHarness" as the product name in all public materials.
- Do not reference DeepSeek Harness or DeepSeek AI in user-facing documentation.
- When describing technical foundations, refer to "open-source Cordis-based agent platform" without naming upstream.
- Brand colors: primary #1E3A8A, accent #F59E0B, neutral #111827.
- Logo and assets are proprietary to DebateHarness Labs.

We aim for a clear and independent brand identity.
