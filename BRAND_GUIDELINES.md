# Artifork Brand Asset Usage Guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

To keep the Artifork ecosystem clear and easy to navigate, avoid user confusion, and make related resources easy to find and identify, we have established these guidelines and hope everyone will follow them:

- In your project's descriptive text, you may use "Artifork" to truthfully and accurately describe your project's relationship with Artifork, for example, "built on Artifork" or "compatible with Artifork". Such descriptions help users understand your project's positioning.
- When naming your own project, please choose a distinct name of your own instead of using "Artifork" itself, so users can tell the two projects apart. Describing the relationship in your README or project description is the clearest way to signal the association.
- Please use the official mark and wordmark only to refer to Artifork itself, and do not alter their proportions, colors, or contents. Do not use official brand materials in promotions or presentations in a way that could cause misunderstanding, so as not to give users the false impression of official endorsement, cooperation, or authorization.
- If you distribute a modified build of Artifork, make clear in your product's own interface and documentation that it is your build based on Artifork, with your own name and branding taking the prominent place.

We believe that a clear and orderly community environment makes every developer's work more visible and more readily recognized. For the few cases that do not follow these guidelines, we may reach out to the relevant parties to make appropriate adjustments in order to maintain the overall order of the ecosystem. Thank you for your understanding and support—let us work together to build a more friendly and sustainable open-source community.
